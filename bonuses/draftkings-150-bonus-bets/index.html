What this offer is, in plain English

Wager $5, Get $150 in Bonus Bets from DraftKings. Sign up, place a qualifying bet of $5 (any market, any odds), and receive $150 (issued as 6 × $25 bonus bets). Bonus credits convert to withdrawable cash only when bets win — and the stake does not return; only the profit on the bet does.

The math, worked out

Place a $5 qualifying bet on Lakers ML at -110. Bet wins or loses — either way, $150 in bonus bets credited as 6 × $25 tokens. Use a $25 bonus bet on Patriots ML at +250. If the Patriots win, the bet pays $25 × 2.5 = $62.50; the $25 stake does not return. Net cash gain: $62.50. If the Patriots lose, the $25 bonus token is gone (no real-money loss because you didn't stake real money).

Expected value

Expected value of $150 in bonus bets is approximately $105-120 depending on usage strategy. Use on plus-money longshots to maximize.

How to maximize the offer

Bonus bets pay only profit, not stake. The expected value of a bonus bet is therefore highest on plus-money odds where the profit-to-stake ratio is high. A $25 bonus bet at +200 returns $50 profit on a win; the same bonus bet at -200 returns just $12.50 profit. Use bonus bets on +150 or longer odds to maximize realized value.
