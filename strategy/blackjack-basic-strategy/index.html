Blackjack Basic Strategy Chart 2026

Blackjack basic strategy is the mathematically optimal play for every possible hand. Memorize it and you reduce the house edge to roughly 0.5% — the lowest of any major casino game.

What is basic strategy?

Basic strategy is a chart specifying the correct decision (hit, stand, double, split, surrender) for every combination of your hand and the dealer's upcard. It's pure math — based on the probability that any given decision will produce the best long-run result.

Key strategy rules

  • Always split aces and 8s
  • Never split 5s or 10s
  • Double on 11 against dealer 2-10
  • Stand on hard 17+; hit hard 11 or below
  • Stand on 12-16 vs. dealer 2-6 (dealer is more likely to bust)
  • Hit 12-16 vs. dealer 7-A
  • Stand on soft 19+; hit soft 17 or below (with double options)

Why it matters

Two players at the same blackjack table can have radically different long-run outcomes — one playing optimally with basic strategy, the other guessing. The chart is widely available; print one, use it at the (virtual) table.

Beyond basic

Once you have basic strategy down, look into card counting (works in person, harder online due to continuous shuffle) and game-specific variant adjustments (single-deck, surrender allowed, etc.).
