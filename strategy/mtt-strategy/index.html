Tournament Poker Strategy: ICM and Push/Fold

Tournament strategy splits into stages: deep stack early game, accumulation middle, ICM-pressure bubble, push/fold final table. Each stage requires different priorities.

Stack depth dictates strategy

Early levels (100+ BBs): plays like deep cash game.

Middle (30-50 BBs): steal and re-steal dynamics.

Bubble (15-25 BBs): ICM pressure peaks. Chip-EV correct play often differs from ICM-correct play.

Final table (12 BBs and under): push/fold equilibrium.

ICM essentials

Independent Chip Model assigns dollar value to remaining chips based on payout structure. A 12-BB stack on a final table is worth less per chip than a 60-BB stack — calling all-in for tournament life requires more equity than pot odds alone suggest.
