NBA Player Props Strategy 2026

NBA player props are the most exploitable market in modern sports betting. Props move slowly when starters are ruled out; sharp bettors get the news first.

The starter-out exploit

When a starter is ruled out, the projection moves but bench-player prop lines often lag the minutes shift by 30-90 minutes. Move fast.

Best NBA prop markets

  • Rebounds for backup centers — historically soft
  • Assists for backup PGs — when starter is out
  • Threes-made for shooters facing high-pace opponents
  • PRA (points + rebounds + assists) for stars in low-rest spots

Track sources

Shams, Wojnarowski, Charania — the three biggest NBA insiders. Following them is essentially mandatory for serious NBA prop betting.
