Golf Betting Strategy: Outrights and Three-Balls

Golf has 144+ players per event. Outrights pay 50/1 to 200/1 — variance is enormous. Three-balls and matchups are the sharper markets.

Course fit beats recent form

Strokes-Gained data shows what skills a course rewards. Augusta = ball striking. TPC Sawgrass = short irons + scrambling. Bay Hill = driving. Match player profile to course profile.

Three-balls

Three players in a featured group; bet which has the lowest score that day. Sharper market than outrights. Correlation with weather wave is huge.

Spread your stakes

Bet 3-6 outrights, not 1. Variance is so high that single-player bets bust 95%+ of the time. Spread reduces variance without killing EV.

Live

Midday cuts — your guy 3-over after 8 holes? Outright price collapses. Hedge cheaply.
