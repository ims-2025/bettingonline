# Week 4 — pokersites.org reciprocal outreach email

**Send date:** on or after 2026-08-18 (after weeks 1-3 have taken hold)
**Recipient:** editorial or partnerships contact at pokersites.org
**Purpose:** ask for a single reciprocal editorial link back to bettingonline.org from one of their high-authority pages

---

## Subject line options (pick one)

- Cross-reference request re: your poker-room directory
- Editorial cross-reference from BettingOnline.org to pokersites.org — following up
- Quick note on a cross-reference we've added

---

## Email body

> Hi [name],
>
> I run editorial at BettingOnline.org — independent operator-review site, poker + sports + casino, active since 2003.
>
> Over the last three weeks I've added three contextual editorial references to pokersites.org from our high-traffic poker pages (`/poker/sites/`, `/poker/bonus/`, `/poker/strategy/`) as a companion resource for readers looking for a broader independent poker-room directory beyond the operators we cover directly. I also published a new pillar resource — a US-Legal Poker Room Directory 2026 at `bettingonline.org/guides/us-legal-poker-2026/` — that references your database alongside the state-level regulator data.
>
> The reason I'm writing: I'd like to propose a single reciprocal contextual link from pokersites.org back to one of our topically-relevant pages. Nothing sitewide, no footer/sidebar placement — just a single editorial reference from one of your existing high-authority pages, where it fits the reader's journey.
>
> A few natural candidates on our side (pick whichever fits your existing content best):
>
> - **`/reviews/` — our poker room reviews hub** — best fit if your target page discusses room-choice or operator evaluation
> - **`/compare/wpn-vs-chico-poker-network/`** — our WPN vs Chico comparison — fits if you have a networks-explained resource
> - **`/guides/us-legal-poker-2026/`** — the new US-legal directory — fits if you have a state-legality resource
> - **`/poker/`** — top-of-funnel poker pillar — safest generic fit
>
> Anchor text is your call; branded ("BettingOnline.org") or descriptive ("independent US operator reviews") both work — we prefer whichever fits your voice naturally.
>
> No pressure, and I've placed our references regardless of whether you reciprocate — the pages we linked from are genuinely improved by pointing readers at your directory. But if there's a natural fit on your side, the reciprocal reference would help both of us signal a real cross-domain editorial relationship (which Google now weights meaningfully in a post-SRA world).
>
> Happy to co-author or contribute editorial content down the line if it makes sense. Let me know if you'd like to chat.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> BettingOnline.org

---

## Notes to keep in mind

- **One reciprocal link is the goal, not many.** More reciprocal placements start reading as owned-network cross-linking and get discounted.
- **Their placement decision is theirs.** Don't specify anchor text or page in prescriptive language — if you dictate, it reads as SEO manipulation and increases the pattern-detection risk.
- **The three unilateral links we've already added stand on their own.** They help their readers regardless of whether pokersites.org reciprocates. That's why the plan is defensible even if the outreach never gets a response.
- **If they reciprocate on one page, don't ask for a second.** Bidirectional relationships work best when they're editorial-cadence, not batch. If a second reference makes sense down the line, they'll add it organically.

---

## After they add the link

- Verify the link is live and do-follow (no `rel="nofollow"` / `rel="sponsored"`).
- Note the URL + anchor text in a link log.
- Wait 4-8 weeks before proposing any further collaboration.

---

## If they don't reply

Don't chase. The three unilateral links stand on their own. A second follow-up 4-6 weeks later is fine; more than that reads as pushy and can damage the relationship for future collaboration.
