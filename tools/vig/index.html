How vig works

The bookmaker prices both sides of a market with built-in margin. -110/-110 means each side has an implied probability of 52.4%, totaling 104.8%. The 4.8% above 100% is the bookmaker's vig.

What's a "good" vig?

Standard NFL/NBA spreads: 4.5%. Soccer 1X2: 5-8%. Player props: 8-15%. Same Game Parlays: 12-25%. Lower vig = better value.
