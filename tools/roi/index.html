Calculate your sports betting return on investment.

ROI (return on investment) measures profit as a percentage of total amount wagered. A bettor with $50,000 wagered and $1,500 profit has 3% ROI. Solid long-term sports bettors run 2-5% ROI.

What's a good ROI?

1-2% ROI: average pro. 3-5% ROI: strong pro. 6%+: rare and usually unsustainable. Most recreational bettors run negative ROI long-term.
