Key findings

  • The fast-vs-slow gap is widening. FanDuel and DraftKings clear instant-bank withdrawals in under an hour on average. The slowest operator (BetRivers) averages 4.5 hours for the same method — nearly 10x slower. The gap was 4x a year ago.
  • Instant bank has taken over as the default fast method. PayPal, historically the fastest US channel, has slowed to 2.5-8.5 hours across operators. Instant-bank transfer via GeoComply's partner network has become materially faster.
  • ACH is stable at 1-4 business days. Every operator is within a reasonable range on ACH; this is the mid-tier method for bettors who don't want to link instant bank.
  • Check by mail is no longer worth recommending. Median clear time is 10-15 business days. Every operator that still offers it does so as an option for users without banking access.

Methodology

Testing is conducted by BettingOnline.org staff using real-money accounts opened in states where each operator is licensed. Each test is a genuine withdrawal — not a request that's later cancelled — with funds actually landing in the destination account.

Sample size: 240 withdrawals in Q2 2026, distributed across 30 per operator (8 operators × 30 tests). Split roughly equally across methods: instant bank (10 per operator), ACH (8), PayPal where supported (6), wire (4), check (2).

Withdrawal amounts: $250, $500, $1,000, $2,500 — representative of typical bettor volumes. Larger withdrawals ($10,000+) can trigger additional manual review; we test those separately in our operator-specific reviews.

Account age: all test accounts are 90+ days old at time of testing. New accounts often experience slower first withdrawals due to enhanced KYC review.

Timing: we record the timestamp of withdrawal request submission and the timestamp of funds credited to the destination account. "Cleared" for ACH means funds available for spending in the destination bank; for instant bank, funds visible in the destination bank's app.

Test spread: withdrawals initiated at varying times of day (morning, midday, evening) and days of week (weekday, weekend). Weekend withdrawals typically take 1-2 additional business days to clear for wire and ACH.

Why this matters for bettors

Withdrawal speed is the single most-complained-about issue in every US sportsbook review corpus we've analyzed. It also has the widest quality variation of any operator-comparison dimension. A bettor moving from BetRivers to FanDuel gets 10x faster withdrawals — a meaningful lifestyle difference for anyone actually cashing out regularly.

Practical implications:

  • Test each operator's first withdrawal with a small amount. Some operators throttle new accounts; some don't. The first-withdrawal experience tells you a lot about what your ongoing experience will be.
  • Method matters more than operator sometimes. If instant bank is available, it beats every other method at every operator. If you must use wire, expect 3-12 business days regardless of operator.
  • Weekends slow things down. A Friday-night ACH request often doesn't clear until Tuesday or Wednesday.
