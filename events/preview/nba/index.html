Top NBA futures markets

Pre-season is when futures markets are softest. Books haven't taken enough sharp action to fully refine prices. The savvy futures bettor identifies value early.

  • Championship/title outright
  • Conference/division winner
  • Team season win totals (over/under)
  • Individual awards (MVP, Rookie of the Year, etc.)
  • To make playoffs (yes/no)

Where to bet NBA futures

DraftKings typically opens earliest with deepest futures menus. FanDuel and BetMGM follow within a few weeks.

Hedging futures

If your futures bet is in contention near the end of the season, you can hedge with a same-side bet to lock in profit. Use our hedge calculator.

Bankroll considerations

Futures lock up your money for months. Don't allocate more than 5-10% of your annual roll to futures markets.

FAQ

When should I bet futures?

Pre-season is the softest market. After 2-3 weeks of regular season, prices have sharpened considerably.

Which book has the best futures prices?

Generally DraftKings and FanDuel post earliest with deepest menus. Always line shop — futures markets vary 5-10 cents across books on the same outcome.
