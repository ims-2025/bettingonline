What most bettors miss

Cash-game bankroll requirements: 25-30 buy-ins for the stake you play. NL50 ($0.25/$0.50) needs a $1,250-$1,500 bankroll. Drop stakes if you lose 5 buy-ins at your current level. This discipline separates profitable cash players from those who bust — even winning-strategy players with insufficient bankrolls go broke to variance.
