What most bettors miss

The Venom's $10M+ guarantee is the largest MTT available to US-based players. The buy-in is $2,650 direct or $22 for the first satellite step. A disciplined satellite path — Step 1 to Step 2 to direct qualifier — can put a $22 investment into a $10M-GTD event with a reasonable hourly-EV expectation. Full-time MTT players build their schedules around big-guarantee series like Venom and OSS Cub3d.
