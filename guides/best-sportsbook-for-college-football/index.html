What most bettors miss

US-regulated books face state-imposed restrictions on college player props (in-state college teams cannot have player props in most regulated states). Offshore books have no equivalent restriction. A Georgia resident wanting to bet on Georgia Bulldogs player props has to route through an offshore book because regulated Georgia operators don't offer that market. This is the single biggest structural edge offshore books hold over regulated ones for CFB bettors.
