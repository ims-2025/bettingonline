What the data shows

Advertised RTP is a theoretical long-run number. On a session-by-session basis, actual return will vary dramatically — a 96% RTP slot can lose 50% in a single session and gain 20% in the next. RTP is meaningful over hundreds of thousands of spins, not over a single evening's play. Low-volatility slots deliver returns closer to RTP faster; high-volatility slots have longer variance windows.
