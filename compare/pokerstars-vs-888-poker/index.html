PokerStars vs 888 Poker: The Two Biggest Online Poker Rooms

PokerStars is the global market leader. 888 Poker is the room with the softest fields and most-generous no-deposit welcome offer. Here's how to choose.

PokerStars and 888 Poker are the two largest legitimate online poker rooms in the world. They suit different player types: PokerStars for serious tournament grinders, 888 for recreational players and beginners. Here's the breakdown.

Player pool

PokerStars has the largest player pool in the world by 3-5×. Tournaments fill faster, cash-game tables are deeper, and stake levels go higher.

888 Poker's player base is smaller but notably softer — more recreational players, fewer hardened pros. For winning players, this matters more than total volume.

Welcome bonuses

PokerStars: $50 free + 100% match up to $600.

888 Poker: $88 free (no deposit required) + 100% match up to $400.

888's no-deposit offer is the strongest in the industry. PokerStars' total dollar value is higher.

Tournament schedule

PokerStars wins decisively. Sunday Million, SCOOP, WCOOP — the biggest weekly and seasonal series anywhere. 888 has a respectable schedule but smaller fields.

Software

PokerStars has more advanced software (Zoom, Spin & Go, Rush format). 888 is solid and getting better but less innovative.

Cash game traffic

PokerStars at high stakes (NL400+). 888 at micro and low stakes (NL2-NL100). Game selection should drive your choice.

Recommendation

Recreational/beginner → 888 Poker. Serious tournament players → PokerStars. Cash-game grinders at low stakes → 888. High-stakes cash → PokerStars. Many serious players use both.
