DraftKings vs BetMGM: Volume vs. Loyalty

DraftKings is the volume leader; BetMGM is the loyalty leader. Here's how they compare across bonuses, app quality, market depth and overall value.

DraftKings and BetMGM serve different sweet spots in the US betting market. DraftKings is the most-used US sportsbook overall — biggest user base, most product velocity, deepest props menu. BetMGM is the loyalty leader with unmatched MGM Resorts integration. Here's how to choose between them.

Welcome offers

DraftKings: Wager $5, Get $150 in Bonus Bets — guaranteed and immediate.

BetMGM: First Bet Offer up to $1,500 — bigger upside if you're betting larger.

For most readers, DraftKings' offer is more useful because it's instant and risk-free at $5.

Product depth

DraftKings has roughly 2× the player-prop volume of BetMGM and a stronger Same Game Parlay+ product. BetMGM has more daily boost markets and a broader Edit Bet implementation.

Payout speed

DraftKings is consistently faster — same-day to 1 day on most methods. BetMGM runs 2-5 days for the same withdrawals.

Loyalty value

BetMGM's MGM Rewards integration is the killer feature. Every wagered dollar earns Tier Credits that convert to real comp value at MGM properties. Over a year of regular betting, this can add 3-5% in tangible value.

Final verdict

If you travel to MGM properties → BetMGM. If not → DraftKings is the safer choice for most users. As always, the smartest move is to open both and line shop.
