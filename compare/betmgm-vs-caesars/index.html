BetMGM vs Caesars: The Loyalty-First Showdown

The two operators with the strongest casino-resort loyalty integrations in US sports betting, head-to-head. If you travel and value comp value, this comparison matters.

BetMGM and Caesars are the two US sportsbooks where loyalty integration is the killer feature. Both convert wagering directly into hotel/dining/show comps at their respective resort networks. If you travel to Las Vegas — or any of 50+ MGM or Caesars properties — the choice between them comes down to which network you use more often.

Welcome bonuses

BetMGM: First Bet Offer up to $1,500. Place a first bet up to $1,500. If it loses, BetMGM credits your stake back in 5 equal bonus-bet increments.

Caesars: Wager $1, Get $300 in Bonus Bets. Lowest entry-cost first-bet promo in the US. Place a $1 bet on any market; receive $300 in bonus bets regardless of result.

Verdict: Caesars wins on accessibility and immediate value. BetMGM wins on dollar-amount potential if you have a large first wager you're confident in.

Loyalty programs (the key differentiator)

BetMGM (MGM Rewards): Tier Credits convert directly to comp value at MGM Resorts properties — Bellagio, MGM Grand, Aria, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM, plus regional properties. Tier levels (Pearl, Gold, Platinum, NOIR) unlock progressively better comp rates and resort benefits.

Caesars (Caesars Rewards): Tier Credits unlock free hotel nights, dining credits, show tickets at Caesars Palace, Harrah's, Horseshoe, Tropicana, Flamingo, Paris, Bally's, and dozens of other properties.

Verdict: Roughly equivalent in scale. The right choice depends on which network you use. If you do most of your Vegas trips at Bellagio or Aria, BetMGM. If you prefer Caesars Palace or Harrah's, Caesars.

Apps and product

BetMGM: Solid app with Lion's Boost daily promos and Edit Bet. Less polished than DraftKings/FanDuel but feature-complete.

Caesars: Recently rebuilt (2023). Clean home screen, working Cash Out. Live streaming is improving but coverage-limited.

Verdict: Roughly equivalent. BetMGM has slightly more daily promotional activity.

Market depth

Both cover NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, soccer, MMA, and golf well. BetMGM has slightly stronger tennis coverage. Caesars frequently posts profit-boost tokens that produce occasionally +EV markets.

Payout speed

BetMGM: 2-5 days for most withdrawals. Slower than DraftKings/FanDuel.

Caesars: 2-4 days. Slightly faster than BetMGM.

Verdict: Slight edge to Caesars. Both noticeably slower than DraftKings or FanDuel.

Final recommendation

This decision is dominated by where you travel. If you frequent MGM properties → BetMGM. If you frequent Caesars properties → Caesars. If you don't travel to either, the loyalty advantage shrinks dramatically and either DraftKings or FanDuel will likely produce more total value for you. Consider a multi-account approach: BetMGM (or Caesars) for the loyalty value, plus DraftKings/FanDuel for the better welcome offers and faster payouts.
