Welcome bonus deep-dive

FanDuel's flagship welcome promo is the No-Sweat First Bet up to $1,000. New users place a single first bet of any size up to $1,000. If it wins, you collect normal winnings plus the bonus is over. If it loses, FanDuel refunds the stake in bonus bets — up to $1,000 — to be played at any odds, on any market.

The strategic move: place your first bet at +200 to +300 odds. If it wins, you've extracted $2,000-$3,000 in cash from a $1,000 stake. If it loses, your bonus bets are worth roughly 70% of face value, meaning your effective EV on the offer is materially positive when you back a fair-priced underdog.

App and UX

The FanDuel app is widely regarded as the cleanest, fastest betting app in the US market. Bet slip behavior is intuitive, search is precise, account management is one-tap. The home screen surfaces same-game parlays, boosted markets and live betting in a way that doesn't feel cluttered.

Live streaming covers all major US sports. Cash Out is available on most pre-match bets. The 'Edit Bet' feature (rolled out in 2024) lets you adjust legs of a parlay after placement — useful for hedging.

Markets and pricing

FanDuel typically posts the most player-prop volume of any US sportsbook, particularly on NFL and NBA. SGP+ allows correlated parlays across player and game lines that traditional SGPs disallow.

Pricing is competitive on standard moneylines, spreads and totals — usually within 1-2 cents of best price. SGPs and SGP+ markets are priced with 12-20% holds, so they should be played for fun, not edge.

Trust and operations

FanDuel is owned by Flutter Entertainment (NYSE: FLUT), one of the world's largest gaming companies. Licensed in every US state where it operates. Funds segregated. Withdrawals via PayPal/Venmo can land same-day; debit card withdrawals typically 1-2 business days.
