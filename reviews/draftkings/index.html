Welcome bonus deep-dive

The DraftKings welcome offer for new sports bettors is currently Wager $5, Get $150 in bonus bets. The mechanic is simple: deposit, opt in, place a $5 first bet on any market with odds of -300 or longer. The $150 in bonus bets credit your account immediately, regardless of whether your first bet wins or loses.

Bonus bets must be used within 7 days of issuance. They convert to cash only if the bonus bet itself wins (the stake is not returned, only the profit). Wagering requirements: each bonus bet must be played through 1× before withdrawal.

Compared to FanDuel's No-Sweat First Bet (which only refunds bonus bets if you lose) and BetMGM's First Bet Offer (which refunds up to $1,500 only if you lose), DraftKings' approach gives you guaranteed bonus bets up front — at the cost of a smaller dollar amount.

App and product experience

The DraftKings app is the most feature-rich in the US market. Same Game Parlay+ lets you build complex multi-leg props within a single game. Quick Bet allows one-tap repeats of a recent wager. Live Streaming covers most US major-league action. Cash Out is universally available.

The app's downside is occasional lag during peak load (Sundays at 1 PM ET, Monday Night Football kickoffs). DraftKings' engineering team has improved this every year, but it's still the single most-cited gripe in user reviews.

Market depth and pricing

DraftKings runs deep on US sports — every major league has full menus including alt lines, team totals, player props, futures, and live in-play markets. International soccer coverage is strong (Premier League, La Liga, Champions League, MLS). UFC and boxing get featured props for major events.

Pricing is competitive but not always the best — particularly on alt-line player props and SGPs, where DraftKings is sometimes a tick worse than FanDuel or BetMGM. Line shopping is essential.

Withdrawals and trust

DraftKings is publicly traded (NASDAQ: DKNG) and operates under licenses in every state where it offers product. Player funds are segregated. Withdrawals to debit cards typically arrive same-day to 1 business day; bank wires take 3-5 days; PayPal and Play+ are usually instant.

Identity verification is required at signup; for larger withdrawals (typically $5,000+), additional documentation is requested.
