Welcome bonus

Caesars' Wager $1, Get $300 is the lowest-entry-cost first-bet promo in the US market. Place a $1 bet on any market; the $300 in bonus bets credit immediately regardless of outcome. Bonus bets are issued as multiple smaller wagers.

Compare to DraftKings' current offer ($5 → $100 in Bonus Bets, paid as four $25 tokens): Caesars wins on headline dollar value, while DraftKings issues the credit instantly regardless of first-bet outcome.

Caesars Rewards integration

The standout feature: every dollar wagered earns Tier Credits and Reward Credits in Caesars Rewards. These convert to free hotel nights, dining, shows and entertainment at every Caesars property — Caesars Palace Vegas, Harrah's, Horseshoe, Tropicana, Flamingo, and more.

Profit-boost tokens are issued frequently to active players — typically 25-50% odds boosts on a chosen single bet, redeemable on any market.

App and product

The Caesars app went through a major rebuild in 2023 (after the William Hill acquisition). It's now solid — clean home screen, clear bet slip, working Cash Out. Live streaming is coverage-limited but improving.

Markets and pricing

Competitive on NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and major soccer/MMA. Mid-tier markets (lower-tier soccer, niche sports, props on minor games) can be thin. Pricing is generally a tick or two off the sharpest US books on standard markets — line shop, especially on player props.
