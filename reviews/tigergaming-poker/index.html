The Chico Poker Network context

The Chico Poker Network is one of two large US-facing offshore poker networks (alongside WPN). The three Chico skins — BetOnline, TigerGaming and Sportsbetting.ag — share a single player pool, cashier and tournament schedule; the differentiators between them are UI, promotions, and cross-vertical integrations with the sportsbook and casino.

Understanding the network matters because your effective player pool, tournament schedule and cash-game liquidity are dictated by network — not by which skin you sign up through. What differs between skins is the promotional structure, the deposit/withdrawal experience, the UI, and (in the case of TigerGaming Poker) the specific welcome offer terms.

The TigerGaming Poker welcome offer

TigerGaming Poker's welcome package is 100% up to $1,000 first deposit + poker rebates, and the mechanics are broadly typical of the Chico model: the match releases incrementally as you generate rake, rather than crediting up-front. Clearance runs on a points-per-dollar basis, and the unclaimed portion expires 30-60 days from first deposit depending on the specific tier of the deposit.

What you should evaluate before claiming any welcome offer:

  • Rake clearance rate. The published number ($1 released per $X rake) determines how much play you need to fully clear the match.
  • Expiry window. Unclaimed match dollars evaporate at the window end. If you can't realistically generate the required rake in the window, the match is smaller than it appears.
  • Bonus stack. TigerGaming Poker runs periodic reload bonuses and freerolls that stack on top of the welcome. If you plan to be a regular player, weigh reload economics as much as the initial match.

Tournaments

The Chico weekly schedule anchors on the Sunday Warm-Up, Sunday $100K GTD and Sunday $50K GTD series, with a full daily MTT and SNG lineup. Tournament fields are smaller than on WPN but the buy-in structures are friendlier for grinders — lower rake, shallower payout curves, and consistent liquidity at low and mid stakes.

The relevant question for tournament-focused players is not "how big is the schedule" — the schedule is deep enough for any bankroll — but "does the schedule fit my time zone." US primetime hours (7pm-1am ET) have the deepest fields; European hours have thinner but still-playable liquidity.

Cash games

Cash games run reliably at NL10 through NL200 with occasional depth up to NL600. The Chico network uses anonymous tables at most stakes — you don't see opponent screen names — which limits third-party tracking software and levels the field for recreational players.

For anyone weighing this against a US-state-regulated room: the offshore-network cash-game experience skews slightly softer at low stakes than the regulated US pools, primarily because the player base includes more international recreational players. Regs at mid and high stakes are just as tough as anywhere.

Software and mobile

The desktop client on TigerGaming Poker is the standard Chico client — competent and functional. The mobile clients (iOS and Android) have improved substantially over the last two years but still lag the best US-regulated apps on polish. Multi-tabling on mobile is possible but not comfortable; serious volume players use desktop.

The client supports the standard third-party tools where allowed — HUDs on WPN, none on Chico due to anonymous tables. Import your hand histories to Hold'em Manager or PokerTracker if you want session review.

Deposits, withdrawals and the cashier

The cashier supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash and a handful of altcoins, plus traditional cards and wire. Crypto withdrawals settle within 1-24 hours; card and wire withdrawals run 5-15 business days depending on method. Poker-only withdrawals are usually processed faster than mixed-vertical withdrawals.

The single most important operational decision on any offshore poker site is method choice. Crypto is faster, cheaper and has higher limits than card or wire, and every serious player who uses these sites eventually moves to crypto for the cashier speed alone.

Rakeback and rewards

The rewards program is a hybrid tier + reload system. Weekly reload bonuses, freeroll tournament tickets, and a rebate program combine to produce effective rakeback of 15-35% depending on volume. The rebate calculation resets weekly, which keeps volume grinders engaged.

For volume players, rakeback often exceeds welcome-bonus value on a lifetime basis. If you're evaluating TigerGaming Poker against another Chico skin (or against Chico), the rakeback structure is a bigger decision factor than the headline welcome number.

Customer support

Support runs 24/7 via live chat and email. Response times on live chat are typically under five minutes for account questions and cashier inquiries. Payment-related issues (identity verification, first withdrawal) can take longer as they route through a KYC review team. Support quality is competent but not exceptional — expect responsiveness on standard issues and slower resolution on anything that requires escalation.

Responsible gambling

TigerGaming Poker provides the standard offshore-market toolset: deposit limits, session-time reminders, and self-exclusion. These are self-service and take effect immediately. The tools are less prescriptive than the state-mandated frameworks at US-regulated rooms — the responsibility falls more heavily on the individual player.

FAQ

Is TigerGaming Poker legal in the US?

TigerGaming Poker operates offshore and accepts US-based players in most states, but it is not licensed by any US state gaming authority. Regulated US-based poker is available in NJ, MI, PA, NV, WV and Delaware; those states have separate licensed operators. Offshore poker exists in a legal grey area — players should understand the jurisdictional context before depositing.
