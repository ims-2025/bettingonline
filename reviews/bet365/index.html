Welcome bonus

The First Bet Safety Net up to $1,000 works similarly to FanDuel's No-Sweat: lose your first bet, get bonus bets equal to your stake, up to $1,000. Bet365 typically credits the bonus bets within 24 hours of the original bet settling.

Bonus bets carry 1× wagering, can be played at any odds, and expire 7 days after issue. As with FanDuel, the strategic play is to place your first bet at +200 to +300 to maximize EV.

Why bet365 is the global standard

Founded in 2000 in Stoke-on-Trent, England, bet365 grew into the world's largest privately-held bookmaker by building the best live-betting product in the industry. The US product launched in 2022 and runs on the same global tech stack — meaning US users get the in-play depth and live streaming that European users have enjoyed for two decades.

For any sport with live action, bet365's in-play menu typically has 2-3× the markets of US-native competitors. Live streaming covers thousands of events globally, including soccer, tennis, NHL, NBA, and select college sports.

App and product

The bet365 app is fast, dense, and feature-rich. The home screen surfaces a customizable list of sports and markets. The bet slip handles complex parlays, system bets, lucky 15s, and round robins. Cash Out, Auto Cash Out, Edit Bet — all available.

UI density can feel overwhelming for new bettors used to FanDuel's minimalism, but the trade-off is unmatched depth.
