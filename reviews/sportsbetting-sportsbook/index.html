Welcome bonus

Sportsbetting.ag's welcome offer is 50% Welcome Bonus up to $1,000 · crypto reload boosters. Like every US-facing offshore welcome, the match releases against a rollover requirement — typically 10-14× the deposit+bonus combined for sports wagering, higher for casino play. Rollover math is the single most important number to verify before you claim any offshore welcome offer. A generous headline match with a punitive rollover is smaller-value than a modest match with a friendly one.

What to check before claiming:

  • Rollover multiplier and eligible bets. Which markets qualify — every straight bet, or only bets at –200 or better?
  • Bonus expiry window. How long you have to hit the rollover before the bonus voids.
  • Max cash-out cap. Some offshore welcomes cap withdrawable bonus winnings.
  • Reload structure. If you plan to be a regular customer, the reload cadence and terms matter more than the headline welcome number.
