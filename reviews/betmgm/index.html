Welcome bonus and loyalty

BetMGM's First Bet Offer up to $1,500 refunds your stake (in bonus bets) if your first wager loses. The bonus credits in 5 equal increments — a $1,500 first bet that loses returns 5x $300 bonus bets, not one $1,500 bonus bet. This caps your re-deployment options but spreads risk.

The bigger story: MGM Rewards integration. Every dollar wagered earns Tier Credits and Reward Credits. Tier Credits qualify you for status (Pearl, Gold, Platinum, NOIR) which converts directly to comp value at MGM properties — Vegas hotels, restaurants, shows. For a player who travels, it can add 3-5% real value to every bet. No other US sportsbook has this loyalty integration.

App and feature set

The BetMGM app is solid — not as slick as FanDuel, but feature-rich. Lion's Boost offers daily odds boosts that are sometimes genuinely +EV (rare in sportsbook boost-land). Edit Bet lets you swap, add or remove legs from a parlay. Easy Parlay auto-builds parlays around a single team's full slate.

Markets and pricing

Strong on NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB. Particularly deep on tennis (ATP/WTA Slam coverage) and European soccer (Premier League, La Liga, Champions League, lower-tier UK leagues). MMA and boxing covered for major events; smaller cards sometimes missing.

Trust and withdrawals

Operated by MGM Resorts International (NYSE: MGM) — one of the most trusted names in gaming. Withdrawals are slower than DraftKings/FanDuel: debit/credit cards typically 2-5 business days; PayPal usually 1-2; bank wire 3-7. Identity verification required at signup.
