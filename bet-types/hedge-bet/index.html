What is a Hedge Bet?

A hedge bet wagers on the opposite side of an existing bet to lock in profit or reduce risk on a hot ticket.

How hedging works

You have a winning bet still in progress. You place a counter-bet at current odds on the opposite side. The combined positions guarantee a known profit/loss regardless of the final outcome.

The classic case

A long-shot futures bet (e.g., $100 on the Chiefs at +800) reaching the Super Bowl. The Chiefs are now -120 in the game. Hedge with a calculated stake on the Eagles to lock in roughly $150 profit either way.
