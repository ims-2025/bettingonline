Bet types

Every Sports Bet Type, Explained

From moneylines to Asian handicaps — every wager you'll encounter at a sportsbook, with worked examples and strategy notes.

The basics

Moneyline

Pick the winner, no spread.

Point Spread

Favorite handicapped by points.

Total (Over/Under)

Combined score over or under.

Multi-leg bets

Parlay

Multiple bets, all must win.

Teaser

Move spreads/totals across multiple games.

Round Robin

Multiple smaller parlays.

If Bet

Conditional: B only if A wins.

Reverse Bet

Bidirectional if-bet.

Hedge

Lock profit by betting opposite side.

Specialty wagers

Prop Bet

Bet on specific events within a game.

Future Bet

Long-term outcomes (championships, MVPs).

Live Betting

In-play wagering as games unfold.

Halftime Bet

Second-half lines, separate from full game.

Asian Handicap

Soccer handicap, no draw outcome.

Draw No Bet

Soccer bet voided on a draw.
