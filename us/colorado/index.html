Colorado sports betting at a glance

Colorado launched mobile sports betting on May 1, 2020, weeks after being one of the first states to authorize the market via voter referendum (Prop DD, November 2019). The state runs a lightly-regulated, competitive-tax environment (10% on AGGR) that has made it one of the most operator-friendly US markets.

Population: 5.9 million. Legal mobile sports betting: yes. Legal retail sports betting: yes. Regulator: Colorado Division of Gaming (part of Department of Revenue).. Tax rate on gross gaming revenue: 10% on adjusted gross gaming revenue (post-promo-deduction)..

Line shopping and hold rates in Colorado

With multiple operators licensed, Colorado residents can and should line-shop every wager. The price difference between operators on a given line can be 1-5 cents on sides and 5-15 cents on props, which compounds meaningfully across a season of betting.

What is the sports-betting tax rate in Colorado?

10% on adjusted gross gaming revenue (post-promo-deduction).. This is a tax on the operator's gross gaming revenue, not on individual player winnings.

Do I have to pay taxes on my Colorado sports-betting winnings?

Yes. Winnings are taxable income at the federal level (reportable on IRS Form W-2G for wins above operator-reporting thresholds) and typically at the state level in the state where the wager was placed. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.
