Georgia sports betting at a glance

Georgia has been repeatedly close to legalizing sports betting since 2022, but the state constitution's prohibition on lotteries has created a legal question about whether legalization requires a constitutional amendment (two-thirds legislative vote + statewide referendum) or can happen via statute alone. This constitutional dispute has been the primary obstacle to passage.

Population: 11.0 million. Legal mobile sports betting: no. Legal retail sports betting: no. Regulator: Georgia Lottery Corporation is the most-proposed regulator. State Gaming Commission would need to be created.. Tax rate on gross gaming revenue: N/A (not legal). Proposals have ranged from 15% to 20%..

Alternatives for Georgia residents

Because Georgia has not yet legalized sports betting, residents' options are limited to:

  • Cross-border wagering — traveling to an adjacent legal state and using a mobile operator there while physically inside that state's geofence. Geolocation requirements make this legal only while in-state.
  • Daily fantasy sports — DraftKings, FanDuel, PrizePicks, Underdog and other DFS operators serve Georgia under DFS-specific statutes distinct from sports betting.
  • Offshore sportsbooks — books licensed outside the US accept Georgia residents but operate outside US state regulatory frameworks. See our independent sportsbook reviews for coverage of the offshore market. Offshore operators do not carry state-regulated consumer protections and cannot be enforced against by the Georgia attorney general in the event of a dispute.
  • Retail casino gaming — Georgia's casinos, where they exist, offer non-sports gaming under separate authorization.

None of these substitute for a licensed, in-state, regulated mobile sportsbook. The gap in consumer protection is meaningful and worth understanding before choosing any of the above.

What Georgia residents can do today

Georgia residents cannot legally place a real-money sports wager within state lines. Some travel to Tennessee (mobile-legal since 2020), North Carolina (mobile since 2024) or Florida (Hard Rock retail + Seminole mobile) to bet. Daily fantasy sports operates in Georgia in a legal grey area. Offshore books accept Georgia residents.

Market size and economic context

Georgia is projected to generate $700M-1.2B in annual handle if legalized — a mid-sized market similar to Tennessee or Arizona.

Market-size projections matter more than headline numbers because they explain the political economy of legalization. States with large projected markets attract more operator lobbying, more legislative attention, and — often — faster passage once the political conditions align. States with smaller projected markets can pass legislation with less controversy but generate less state revenue post-launch.

Public opinion in Georgia

AJC 2025 poll: 60% support, 30% oppose. Support is broadest among sports-fan demographics and weakest among rural evangelical voters.

Responsible gambling in Georgia

Can I bet legally by crossing into a neighboring state?

Yes — mobile sportsbooks in adjacent legal states use geolocation to enforce in-state wagering. If you are physically inside the geofence of a mobile-legal state at the moment you place a bet, and you have registered an account with that operator, the wager is legal in that state's jurisdiction.
