Ohio sports betting at a glance

Ohio launched a full sports-betting market on January 1, 2023 following HB 29's passage in December 2021. The market operates a three-tier license structure: Type A (mobile, up to 25 skins), Type B (retail sportsbook), and Type C (kiosk at bars and restaurants). Ohio's tax rate was doubled from 10% to 20% in July 2023 during Governor DeWine's operating-budget process — one of the fastest tax-rate hikes in US regulated sports-betting history.

Population: 11.8 million. Legal mobile sports betting: yes. Legal retail sports betting: yes. Regulator: Ohio Casino Control Commission (OCCC).. Tax rate on gross gaming revenue: 20% on adjusted gross gaming revenue (raised from 10% in July 2023)..

What Ohio residents can do today

Ohio residents 21+ may legally place mobile and retail sports wagers with any Type A operator. In-state geolocation required. Kiosk (Type C) wagering available at more than 1,300 licensed bars and restaurants — a distinctive Ohio feature.

Line shopping and hold rates in Ohio

With multiple operators licensed, Ohio residents can and should line-shop every wager. The price difference between operators on a given line can be 1-5 cents on sides and 5-15 cents on props, which compounds meaningfully across a season of betting.

What is the sports-betting tax rate in Ohio?

20% on adjusted gross gaming revenue (raised from 10% in July 2023).. This is a tax on the operator's gross gaming revenue, not on individual player winnings.

Do I have to pay taxes on my Ohio sports-betting winnings?

Yes. Winnings are taxable income at the federal level (reportable on IRS Form W-2G for wins above operator-reporting thresholds) and typically at the state level in the state where the wager was placed. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

Can I bet on college sports in Ohio?

Ohio's regulator sets the specific rules on in-state college prop restrictions. Most states restrict player props on games involving in-state college teams. Check the operator's market list for the current rules.
