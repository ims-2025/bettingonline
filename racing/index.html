Bet Types

The core Racing markets:

  • Moneyline: pick the winner outright. The cleanest market and often the sharpest pricing.
  • Spread / handicap: the favorite is asked to win by more than X.5. Smooths lopsided matchups.
  • Total (over/under): bet on combined points/goals/runs.
  • Player props: individual stat-line bets. Often the softest pricing in the market.
  • Live betting: in-play markets that move with the action. Fast clickers with a strong read can find real edge.

Racing Betting Strategy

Three principles that work for nearly every sport:

1. Line shop everything. Same line at three books often varies by 5-10 cents. The compounding edge over a season is enormous.
