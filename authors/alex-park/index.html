Background

Former trading-desk analyst at a Tier-1 European sportsbook. 12 years in the industry. Specializes in NFL, NBA player props, and bankroll analytics. As Editor-in-Chief at BettingOnline.org, Alex is responsible for editorial decisions in the editor category — including operator reviews, strategy articles, and quarterly re-scores.

Areas of expertise

Alex's primary specializations include sports betting market analysis, sportsbook product evaluation, bankroll mathematics, and the application of expected-value frameworks to recreational and professional betting.

Editorial principles

Every piece Alex contributes to BettingOnline.org follows our methodology — real-money testing, no paid placement, transparent affiliate disclosures, and quarterly re-scoring of operator quality.
