Background and credentials

Chris Thompson spent four years (2019-2023) as a counselor at a state-funded problem gambling treatment program, working directly with individuals affected by gambling-related harm and their families. The role involved 1:1 counseling, group facilitation, and outreach to local communities about problem gambling resources.

Chris joined BettingOnline.org in 2023 as Responsible Gambling Lead. The role focuses on ensuring BettingOnline.org's content models healthy betting behavior, presents responsible-gambling resources prominently, and avoids any content that could exacerbate harm for vulnerable users.

Chris holds an M.S.W. (Master of Social Work) and is a Certified Problem Gambling Counselor (CPGC) — the credentialing certification administered by the National Council on Problem Gambling. Chris completes 20+ hours of continuing education annually on gambling-related harm.

Editorial principles & disclosures

Chris follows the BettingOnline.org editorial standards. Content under Chris's byline never minimizes gambling-related harm or implies that problem gambling is a personal failing. Where applicable, content directs readers to the 1-800-GAMBLER helpline (operated by the National Council on Problem Gambling) and to state-specific resources.

To prevent conflicts of interest, Chris does not currently practice clinically. He maintains his certifications as part of his professional development. His clinical training informs his editorial perspective without creating client-side obligations.

Chris has no equity, advisory, or other relationships with any operator or with BettingOnline.org's parent company beyond standard editorial-team compensation. Any conflicts of interest related to specific organizations or programs are disclosed at the article level.

If betting stops being fun

Help is free, confidential, and available 24/7. Call 1-800-GAMBLER in the US, visit ncpgambling.org, or use your state's specific helpline (linked from our responsible gambling resources page).
