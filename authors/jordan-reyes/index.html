Background

Quant background. Built the underlying math for our calculators and runs our weekly EV-tracking workflow. As Data & Tools Lead at BettingOnline.org, Jordan is responsible for editorial decisions in the data category — including operator reviews, strategy articles, and quarterly re-scores.

Areas of expertise

Jordan's primary specializations include sports betting market analysis, sportsbook product evaluation, bankroll mathematics, and the application of expected-value frameworks to recreational and professional betting.

Editorial principles

Every piece Jordan contributes to BettingOnline.org follows our methodology — real-money testing, no paid placement, transparent affiliate disclosures, and quarterly re-scoring of operator quality.
