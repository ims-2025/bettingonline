Background and credentials

Jordan Reyes has spent eight years working at the intersection of live betting markets and sportsbook product design. From 2017 to 2022, Jordan worked as a quantitative analyst at a major sportsbook software provider, focusing on live-pricing models for NBA, NFL, and major-tournament soccer markets. The role involved building and maintaining algorithmic pricing systems used by multiple operator clients.

Jordan transitioned to BettingOnline.org in 2023 as Live Betting & Product Analyst. The role focuses on covering the rapidly-growing live betting segment of US sports betting markets and providing product-level analysis of how operators design and price their in-play offerings.

Jordan holds a B.S. in Statistics and an M.S. in Quantitative Finance. They have presented at industry conferences on live-pricing model architecture and have authored white papers on micro-betting market design.

Coverage areas

  • Live betting strategy. Strategy content covering in-play markets, including timing, market selection, and the structural margin differences between pre-game and live markets.
  • Live betting industry trends. Coverage of the expansion of in-play wagering as a percentage of total handle, and how operators differentiate on live betting product features.
  • eSports betting. Coverage of major eSports betting markets (LoL, CS2, Valorant, Dota 2) — Jordan's quantitative background suits the data-rich, frequent-update structure of competitive gaming.
  • Player props and micro-betting markets. Strategy and market-structure analysis for the rapidly-expanding player-prop and micro-bet segments.

Editorial principles & disclosures

Jordan follows the BettingOnline.org editorial standards. Strategy content under Jordan's byline is informed by direct quantitative analysis of public market data (operator-published results, AGA aggregated data, third-party industry research from Eilers & Krejcik Gaming and similar). Where claims are based on Jordan's prior operator-side experience, that origin is disclosed in the relevant article.

Jordan maintains personal accounts at major US sportsbooks for review purposes. To prevent conflicts of interest with their prior operator-side role, Jordan does not write coverage involving the specific operator-software platform they worked on, and any tangentially-related coverage is reviewed by Alex Park before publication.

Jordan has no equity, advisory, or other relationships with any operator or with BettingOnline.org's parent company beyond standard editorial-team compensation.
