Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

BettingOnline.org collects only what we need to operate the site:

  • Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized page views and engagement metrics via privacy-respecting analytics. We do not use Google Analytics.
  • Newsletter signups: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We do not share or sell email addresses.
  • Contact form submissions: Stored only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry.

We do not require user accounts. We do not collect betting activity, identity documents, or financial information.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for site functionality:

  • Theme preference cookie: stores your dark/light mode choice (first-party, no third parties)
  • Affiliate tracking cookies: set by operators when you click through our links — these belong to the operator, not us

You can clear or disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to read our content.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click them and sign up at the operator, we may receive a commission. The cookies set by these links are first-party to the operator's domain — they track only your interaction with that operator, not your activity on our site or other sites.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including but not limited to GDPR for EU residents and CCPA for California residents), you have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email privacy@bettingonline.org.

Children

Our site is intended for users 21 and over (18 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.

Contact
