Reading pay tables

Look at the Full House and Flush payouts on Jacks or Better. 9/6 Jacks or Better (9 for full house, 6 for flush, both per credit on a 5-coin bet) returns 99.54% with optimal play. 8/5 Jacks or Better returns 97.30%. 7/5 Jacks returns 96.15%. Same game, dramatically different RTP.

Optimal strategy basics

Always play 5 coins to qualify for the royal flush bonus (4,000-coin payout vs. 1,000 for a 4-coin bet). Hold any pair higher than 10s; hold three to a royal flush; hold four-card flush draws. Strategy charts for each variant are widely available — print one, follow it, beat the average player.

Why video poker beats slots

Slots are pure RNG: nothing you do affects the outcome. Video poker has skill: the cards you hold determine what you draw. Two players on the same machine can have very different long-run results.
