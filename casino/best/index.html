RTP and house edge by game type

Return to Player (RTP) is the long-run percentage of wagered money the game returns to players. House edge is 100% minus RTP. RTP varies dramatically by game type:

  • Slots: 88-98% RTP depending on the specific game. Average across major slot libraries: 95-96%. Progressive jackpot slots have lower base RTPs (88-92%) with the difference contributing to the jackpot pool.
  • Blackjack: 99.5% RTP with basic strategy on standard 6-deck blackjack at standard rules (S17, double after split, surrender allowed).
  • European roulette: 97.3% RTP (single zero house edge 2.7%).
  • American roulette: 94.7% RTP (double zero house edge 5.26%).
  • Baccarat: 98.94% RTP on banker bet, 98.76% on player bet, 85.6% on tie bet.
  • Craps: varies by bet — pass/come at 98.6% RTP with full odds; proposition bets at 89-94% RTP.
  • Video poker: 95-100.7% RTP depending on game and pay table. 9/6 Jacks-or-Better at 99.5% with optimal play.
  • Live dealer game shows: 95-97% RTP.

No. State-licensed US online casinos are regulated by state gaming authorities and use certified random number generators. Game RTP percentages are audited and published. Avoid offshore casinos which have no regulatory oversight.

How much can I withdraw from a US online casino?

Can I count cards at online blackjack?

No. Online blackjack uses a continuous shuffle machine that re-shuffles after every hand. Card counting is impossible. Live dealer blackjack uses real cards but the deck is also shuffled frequently enough to prevent counting at scale.

Are slot RTPs honest at US online casinos?

Yes. State regulators require operators to publish RTP for every slot. RTPs are verified by independent testing labs and audited periodically.
