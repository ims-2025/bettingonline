Editorial methodology

How We Research and Verify

Our process for producing operator reviews, strategy content, and original research.

Operator reviews

Every operator review reflects hands-on testing, not desk research. Our review process:

  1. Sign-up flow. We open a real account in a state where the operator is legal, complete identity verification, and document the experience.
  2. Deposit and withdrawal. We deposit, place test bets, and request a withdrawal. We measure speed, friction, and any complications.
  3. Product testing. Across 30+ days, we place bets on game lines, props, parlays, SGPs, and live markets — comparing pricing to other operators using closing-line data.
  4. Customer support. We test live chat, phone, and email channels, and document response times and quality.
  5. Responsible gambling tooling. We test deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, and reality checks at the operator level.
  6. Compliance and licensing review. We verify licensing across every state where the operator claims to operate.

Strategy content

Strategy guides are written by working bettors, reviewed by our editorial team, and stress-tested with public data where possible. Where we make claims about edge, hold percentages, or market dynamics, we cite operator filings, AGA reports, state regulator data, or our own market sampling.

News and industry coverage

News articles draw from primary sources: SEC filings, state regulator releases, AGA publications, operator earnings calls, and verified industry-research firms (Eilers & Krejcik, Wells Fargo Gaming Equity, Macquarie). We avoid unsourced industry rumor.

Original research

Our annual State of the Industry report synthesizes Q1 operator filings, AGA data, state regulator monthly reports, and our internal market sampling across 200+ markets at 6 major operators. The methodology is documented within the report itself.

Fact-checking

Every claim that's specific, numerical, or attributable is fact-checked against primary sources. Where data is contested or evolving, we present multiple estimates and date the data point.

Update cadence

  • Welcome offers and promo content: reviewed weekly, refreshed monthly.
  • Operator reviews: reviewed quarterly, refreshed at least every six months.
  • State guides: refreshed at every regulatory change in that state, plus a quarterly review.
  • Strategy content: refreshed when underlying math, tools, or market structure changes materially.
  • News: published continuously; corrections made within 24 hours of identification.

Corrections policy

If we make a factual error, we correct it. Corrections are noted in the article with a date stamp. Major corrections are also disclosed in our news section.

Sources we trust

  • American Gaming Association (AGA) — handle, GGR, market data
  • State regulator filings — monthly reports from NJ, NY, PA, IL, MA, etc.
  • Operator SEC and 10-Q filings — public-company financial data
  • National Council on Problem Gambling — responsible gambling resources
  • Eilers & Krejcik Gaming — industry analysis and operator share data
  • Tax court rulings and IRS guidance — tax-related content

What we don't use

  • Anonymous tipster claims
  • Operator-paid 'reports'
  • Tout-service track-record claims
  • Unsourced industry rumor sites

Editorial independence

Our affiliate relationships are disclosed (disclosure) and do not influence ratings or editorial. The editorial team and the business team operate separately. Operators cannot pay for placement, ranking, or to remove negative coverage.
