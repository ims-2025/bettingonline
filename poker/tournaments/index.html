Tournaments

Online Poker Tournaments

Buy-in once. Play until you bust or win it all. Tournaments are the lottery-tickets of poker — high variance, occasional life-changing scores, and the format that built every televised poker boom.

Tournaments offer poker's biggest payouts and biggest volatility. A skilled MTT regular might play 1,000+ tournaments in a year and have only 4-5 deep runs that account for most of their profit. The format requires patience, ICM awareness, and the ability to switch gears as blinds grow and stack-to-pot ratios collapse.

Tournament formats

MTT (Multi-Table Tournament): hundreds to thousands of players, deep structures, runs 4-12+ hours. SNG (Sit & Go): single-table, starts when full, 60-90 minutes. Spin & Go / Jackpot SNGs: 3-handed hyper-turbo with random prize-pool multipliers (up to 1,000,000×). Satellite: tournaments where seats to bigger events are awarded.

Stack depth dictates strategy

Early levels (100+ BBs) play like a deep cash game. Middle stages (30-50 BBs) are about steal/re-steal dynamics. Bubble (15-25 BBs) brings ICM pressure — the chip-EV-correct play often differs from the ICM-correct play. Final table (12 BBs and under) becomes a push/fold equilibrium.

ICM — the math of late stage

Independent Chip Model assigns dollar value to each tournament chip based on remaining payouts. A 12-BB stack on a final table is worth less per chip than a 60-BB stack — so calling all-in for your tournament life requires more equity than the pot odds suggest. Apps like ICMIZER and Holdem Resources Calculator are essential study tools. For tournament-specific video training that walks through ICM spots hand by hand, Deuces Cracked has one of the deepest MTT course libraries online.

Bankroll for MTTs

Tournaments are higher-variance than cash games. Recommended: 100+ buy-ins for your typical MTT field size, 200+ for large fields. Don't play above your roll — the variance will bury you long before your edge materializes.

Tournaments FAQ

Where can I play Tournaments online?

PokerStars, 888 Poker, partypoker and WSOP.com (US-regulated) all spread Tournaments regularly. Check our top-of-page rankings for current bonus offers.

What stakes should I start at?

Start at the smallest stakes the site offers. Even strong live players are often surprised by online aggression and pace. Build comfort and bankroll before moving up.

Do I need tracking software?

Not to start. Once you're playing 5,000+ hands a month, software like Hold'em Manager 3 or PokerTracker 4 will pay for itself many times over by surfacing your leaks.
