Online Poker Strategy: The Fundamentals

Online poker is a skill game that pays off discipline, patience, and study. The 5% of players who put in the work consistently win money from the 95% who don't.

1. Position is power

Late position (button, cutoff) is worth more than your cards in many situations. You see opponents act first and can play more hands profitably. Tighten up dramatically out of position; widen up in late position.

2. Pre-flop ranges, not specific hands

Modern poker thinks in terms of ranges — the distribution of hands you'd play this way. Open from UTG with the top 12-15% of hands; from the button you can open 40%+. Memorize a starting-range chart for each position.

3. Bankroll management is non-negotiable

Cash games: 25 buy-ins for the stake you're playing. Tournaments: 100+ buy-ins. Drop down a level if you lose 5 buy-ins; move up only when you have 30+ buy-ins of the next level. Most players who go broke do it by playing above their bankroll.

4. Solver work pays

Modern training uses GTO solvers (PioSolver, GTO Wizard, Holdem Resources Calculator). You don't need to memorize Nash equilibrium — but understanding the why behind common postflop spots will lift your win rate by 2-3 BB/100.

5. Game selection beats game theory

The fastest way to win at poker: play against worse players. Don't rejoin the same table where one opponent has been crushing for an hour. Hunt softer fields — recreational sites like 888, low-stakes Bovada, evening hours when leisure players log in.

6. Tilt control is a trainable skill

One of the most reliable predictors of long-term success: the ability to walk away after a bad beat. Set stop-loss rules. Take frequent breaks. Don't play impaired or exhausted.

7. Track everything

Hand-tracking software (Hold'em Manager 3, PokerTracker 4) lets you see your win rate, leaks, and weak spots. Replay big losing hands. Discuss with stronger players. The compounding effect over years is enormous.

Online Poker FAQ

Is online poker legal in my state?

Online poker is regulated and legal in NJ, PA, MI, NV, WV and Delaware. Multi-state pools (MSIGA) allow shared liquidity between NJ, NV, MI and PA. Outside the US, it's regulated in the UK, EU, Canada (Ontario) and many parts of LATAM and Asia.

Can I really win money playing online poker?

Yes — but it takes work. The industry estimates 10-15% of regular online players are long-term winners. The rest lose to rake (the operator's commission) and to the winners. Study, bankroll discipline, and game selection are essential.

What's rakeback?

Rake is the operator's commission on cash-game pots. Rakeback returns a percentage (typically 20-40%) of your contributed rake. For volume players, rakeback is often the difference between break-even and a profitable year.

Tournaments or cash games?

Tournaments offer huge upside (a single win can equal months of grinding) but high variance — many losing months for one big score. Cash games are steadier income but require deeper study and stricter game selection.

What software do I need?

For learning: any solver-based course (Run It Once, Upswing, Solve For Why). For tracking: Hold'em Manager 3 or PokerTracker 4. For solver work: GTO Wizard or PioSolver. None are mandatory; all help.
