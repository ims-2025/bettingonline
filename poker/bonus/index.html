Strategy Fundamentals

Position is power. Late position lets you act with more information. Tighten ranges out of position; widen on the button.

Range-based thinking. Modern poker analyzes hands as ranges of possibilities, not specific holdings. Solver work (PioSolver, GTO Wizard) builds that intuition.

Bankroll management. Cash: 25 buy-ins for the stake. Tournaments: 100+ buy-ins. Drop down on a 5-buy-in loss.

Game selection beats game theory. The fastest way to win at poker is to play against weaker players.
